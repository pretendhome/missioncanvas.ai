The company

AI is only useful if you can trust it with your actual information. Today most people can't.

Regulated companies in healthcare, legal, finance and construction want AI on their real work, but they can't send client files, patient records or financials to a cloud model, or prove where the data went once a model has seen it. Everyone else is told to hand over their data, pay a subscription and learn to prompt. Mission Canvas answers both with one product line.

How it works

Know what a request is before you let AI act on it.

At Alexa I learned that the hard part of agents is knowing what a request is before acting on it. Mission Canvas makes that check structural: it is built in, not a setting, and it is patent-pending.

01AskA person asks in plain language, about their own files, email or records.
→
02ClassifyThe request is placed on a 312-node map of the work before any model sees it.
→
03KeepSensitive work stays on the machine. Nothing leaves without permission.
→
04ProveEvery answer carries a receipt: what was used, what was blocked, what left.
In production

A canvas for each industry, each one born inside a real customer's operations.

A canvas is the set of apps a business runs its work on, set up for that business and handed over. All of them run on Palette, our desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux.

Construction · Livella

Job site, office and fleet in one system

Reads delivery tickets and matches them, keeps pour logs and safety declarations compliant, tracks fleet and fuel by machine and job. Runs every day at a construction firm of about 250 people, from the office to the field crews.

Healthcare

Emergency-room prioritization

Helps staff prioritize emergency-room patients with patient data kept where it belongs. A regional public health authority is rolling it out to about 80 hospital staff.

Voice & Front Desk

AI that answers the phone and WhatsApp

Books, reschedules and follows up, with caller verification. It started in clinics and is used by about 10 small businesses, each with 3 to 10 users.

Education · Lingua Viva

An AI app for teachers

Turns classroom notes into tomorrow's differentiated lesson, with student data kept on the school's own device. Tested at 9 schools in an international school network.

Logistics

Order tracking with an overnight risk check

Order tracking for a hardware distributor, with a risk check that runs overnight on its own machines.

Palette

The app for everyone

Point it at a folder. It reads only what you allow, on your own computer, and links every line to its source. About 85 downloads since its October 21 launch, with no marketing yet.

Real work, running today. We decided to start a company when the product got real customers, not before.
How we deliver

From the way a business works to software it can run, in four to six weeks.

We don't start from zero for each client. We start from the working canvas for the industry, change it to fit, and build only the parts that business needs.

01 · Audit

Map how work really moves: the tools, the files, the handoffs. Measure where the hours go.

02 · Redesign

Cut the steps that only exist to move information from one system to another.

03 · Assemble

Start from the industry's working app, change it to fit, build what only this business needs.

04 · Operate

The software takes over repeat work. We measure it and adjust it with the team.

How I lead

A lean company, built under the same governance we sell.

I direct a team of 3 to 11 contract AI engineers, depending on the work, and much of Mission Canvas was built by teams of AI agents I orchestrate. The method is what lets a small team ship like a large one without losing the thread.

The improvement loop for the product
1ObserveA walk-through, a teardown, a usage study, a prospect's question.
→
2Job"When …, I want to …, so I can …"
→
3HypothesisWhat would change the behaviour.
→
4ComponentOne named part of the system.
→
5TestWhat would show it worked.
No change without a component and a test. A finding that can't name a part of the system is a marketing finding, and it goes to marketing.

Verify, don't assert

We only claim what we checked. "Cannot tell" beats a confident guess, in the product's answers and in our own status reports.

Fix the class, not the instance

A problem that comes back is a structural problem. We close the category with a test that locks it, then move on.

The customer's machine is the boundary

The product never depends on a cloud model to work. Local first is a design rule, not a pricing tier.

The record

I use communication to make lives better.

The thread through all of it is language, and specifically how words act. J. L. Austin's speech act theory says some words don't just describe the world, they do something in it: "I promise", "I resign", "Book me a table". That turned out to be exactly the problem with AI agents.
