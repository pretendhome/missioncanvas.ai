About Mission Canvas

Designing the future of human–AI collaboration.

Mission Canvas exists to help every person and every organization design, understand, and continuously improve the work that matters most — through AI they can actually answer for.

Our mission

AI should not replace human expertise. It should amplify it.

We are building toward a world where intelligent systems help people spend less time searching, organizing, and repeating work — and more time creating, solving problems, caring for others, and making decisions that matter.

From AI that you visit to AI that executes the work around you. Requests run on hardware the customer controls, with governed handoffs to frontier models only when they add value. The user decides what runs where, what information leaves, which models are used, and why.

Our vision

Every important workflow should be visible, improvable, and trusted.

Spreadsheets transformed finance. Computer-aided design transformed engineering. Mission Canvas aims to do the same for knowledge work — a system where people can see how work flows, understand how decisions were made, and improve those workflows over time.

Whether someone is organizing their personal life, running a small business, managing a hospital, or coordinating a global enterprise, the requirement is the same. AI should not be a black box. It should be explainable, governed, collaborative, and trusted.

What we believe

Knowledge work deserves the innovation manufacturing already got.

Today, the knowledge an organization depends on is scattered across systems that do not talk to each other. People spend hours every day looking for information they already have.

Email Calendars Documents Cloud storage Messaging Databases Business applications Personal notes

Mission Canvas connects that knowledge into a living system so people and organizations can think more clearly, work together more effectively, and continuously improve. We believe every organization should own its workflows rather than rent its intelligence.

Transforming healthcare

Clinicians spend too much time on administration and not enough on patients.

Healthcare is one of the largest opportunities for responsible AI, and one of the hardest places to deploy it. Patient information cannot be pasted into a public model, and the proposed update to the HIPAA Security Rule would require documented evidence behind decisions that touch protected health information.

That is the problem Mission Canvas was built for. Sensitive domains are routed to a local model by rules compiled into the system, and every answer carries a record of the path it took. Mission Canvas helps healthcare organizations redesign administrative and clinical workflows that improve care while reducing unnecessary cost.

Reduce administrative burden

Automate repetitive documentation, scheduling, referrals, prior authorization, and operational workflows.

Reduce clinician burnout

Let physicians, nurses, and care teams spend more time with patients and less time navigating fragmented systems.

Improve patient outcomes

Support earlier diagnosis, better care coordination, fewer errors, and more consistent follow-up.

Expand access to AI

Bring enterprise-grade AI workflow capability to community hospitals, rural health systems, federally qualified health centers, specialty clinics, and independent practices — without requiring an enterprise engineering team.

Improve affordability

Increase operational efficiency so more resources go to patient care rather than administrative overhead.

Accelerate medical innovation

Help researchers and healthcare leaders spend less time assembling information and more time testing better models of care.

Looking ahead

We are not simply building AI software.

We are building infrastructure for a different way of working. A future where people and AI collaborate naturally. Where knowledge is connected instead of fragmented. Where organizations learn continuously instead of repeatedly starting over. Where intelligent workflows become reusable assets that improve over time.

Our aspirations are deliberately large:

Our north star
